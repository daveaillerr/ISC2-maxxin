# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students preparing for ISC2 certification exams (e.g., CC, CISSP), using the app as a primary study resource and practice tool.

## Product Purpose
To provide realistic exam simulation, quiz exercises, progress tracking, and study resources to help candidates successfully pass the ISC2 CC exam certification.

## Positioning
An all-in-one preparation hub that combines test simulation, progress analytics, and structured study resources tailored for ISC2 certifications.

## Operating Context
Users typically access the app in focused study sessions, taking practice exams, reviewing correct/incorrect answers, and checking glossary terms to reinforce knowledge.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Exam simulation and quiz modes with scoring
- Progress tracking and results visualization
- Glossary and topic reviewer resources
- Built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and React Router

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing page structures (`ExamHub`, `ExamSession`, `ExamResults`, `Glossary`, `Reviewer`, `Home`)
- Data definitions for exams and questions in `src/data` and `src/data_json`

## Product Principles
- **Realistic simulation:** Mirror the actual exam format to build user confidence.
- **Actionable feedback:** Provide clear insights into progress and areas needing review.
- **Accessible knowledge:** Make study materials and definitions easy to find.
